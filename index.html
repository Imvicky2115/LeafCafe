Industrial heritage softened by art, lanterns & greenery.

Created by the design studio Advanced Nature, Leaf transforms an industrial warehouse in Hadapsar into a warm, breathable sanctuary. Hand-crafted hanging lamps, upcycled wooden tables, and thriving indoor foliage make it a favorite spot for quiet work, weekend brunches, and community art gatherings.
